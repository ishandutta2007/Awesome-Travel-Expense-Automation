# Awesome-Travel-Expense-Automation

## Top Travel Expense Automation Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Corporate Travel Expenses, Expense Reporting, Receipt Automation, Reimbursements, Corporate Cards & Spend Management*  
**Last updated: August 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Travel Expense Automation**. These tools automate employee expense capture, receipt processing, expense reports, approvals, reimbursements, corporate cards, travel spending, policy enforcement, accounting integration, and spend analytics.

**Examples** include SAP Concur, Ramp, Brex, Navan Expense, Emburse, Expensify, Zoho Expense, Pleo, Soldo, and Moss (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with open-source expense-management applications, accounting platforms, self-hosted expense trackers, receipt-processing projects, and financial automation building blocks. Fully featured open-source equivalents to enterprise platforms such as Concur, Ramp, or Brex remain relatively uncommon, so the list includes projects that can be combined to build custom self-hosted expense-management systems.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites or GitHub repositories.

## Building a Custom Open-Source Expense Automation Stack

A practical open-source travel-expense platform can be assembled from several layers:

1. **Employee expense capture** — New Expensify, ExpenseOwl, Expensave, or a custom mobile/web application.
2. **Receipt ingestion** — Mobile camera upload, email ingestion, file uploads, or corporate-card transaction feeds.
3. **OCR** — Tesseract, PaddleOCR, or another open-source document-processing engine.
4. **Expense extraction** — Extract merchant, date, amount, currency, tax, category, payment method, and receipt metadata.
5. **Expense classification** — Rules, machine-learning models, or LLM-based categorization.
6. **Policy engine** — Build configurable rules for per-diem limits, receipt requirements, mileage, categories, locations, and employee roles.
7. **Approval workflow** — Frappe/ERPNext, New Expensify, n8n, Node-RED, or a custom workflow engine.
8. **Corporate-card ingestion** — Integrate card/bank transaction feeds through APIs or financial-data connectors.
9. **Reconciliation** — Match receipts and employee-submitted expenses against card transactions.
10. **Reimbursements** — Generate approved reimbursement records and connect them to accounting/payment systems.
11. **Accounting** — ERPNext, Akaunting, Frappe Books, or another open-source accounting platform.
12. **Storage** — MinIO or S3-compatible object storage for receipts and supporting documents.
13. **Analytics** — PostgreSQL, Grafana, Apache Superset, or Metabase.
14. **Automation** — n8n, Node-RED, or Apache Airflow for scheduled and event-driven workflows.
15. **Audit trail** — Store immutable expense history, approval events, policy decisions, and accounting changes.

The open-source ecosystem is particularly strong in accounting, expense tracking, receipt OCR, workflow automation, and self-hosted financial management, while fully integrated open-source equivalents of SAP Concur, Ramp, Brex, Navan, or Emburse remain relatively limited. The strongest approach is therefore generally to combine specialized open-source projects into a unified expense-automation platform rather than expect one project to replace an enterprise T&E suite end-to-end.

Disclaimer

This is a community-curated list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

Some projects listed under Open-Source are complete expense applications, while others are accounting systems, personal-finance applications, receipt-processing tools, workflow engines, or financial infrastructure.

Personal-finance and expense-splitting applications should not automatically be considered direct replacements for enterprise travel-and-expense platforms.

Open-source components may require substantial integration work before they can provide the functionality, corporate-card integrations, travel booking, policy enforcement, tax handling, compliance, and accounting connectivity of commercial enterprise platforms.

Expense systems may process sensitive financial and employee information and should be properly secured, audited, and access-controlled.

Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper hardening, monitoring, backups, patching, and operational support.
